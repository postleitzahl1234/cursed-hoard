**D-01. Factions** — **4 buildable families:** Orc, Crypt (undead), Demon, Goblin. **Witch & Beast cut.** Model is *world-themed, all buildable*: each world favours one signature family (its tiles/lore), but every unlocked family stays buildable on every map. · 2026-05-29

**D-02. Tower upgrade tree** — universal **6-tier, two-split** tree, identical shape for all 4 factions:
- **T1** base · **T2** base upgrade (generic, no choice yet).
- **First split** → 2 specializations; pick one. Each has **T3 (I)** + **T4 (II)**.
- **Second split** → **3 terminal sub-specs**: one exclusive to each first-spec + one **shared "middle"** reachable from either side. Your first-split choice gates you to **2 of the 3** terminals. Each terminal has **T5 (I)** + **T6 (II)**.
- Worked example (Crypt): `Crypt I → Crypt II →` choose **Frost** or **Fear** `→ Frost Crypt I/II →` then **College of Frost Engineering** (frost-only) · **Lightning Crypt** (shared) · *Terror terminal* (fear-only) `→ I/II`.
- **No "T5-combined" finale, no T6 fusion/merge** (both aborted).
- Sprite count: **12 distinct tower silhouettes / faction** (2 base + 2×2 first-spec + 3×2 terminal) × 4 = **48 towers**. · 2026-05-29

**D-03. Tiers** — **6 tiers total.** Tower fusion/merging aborted. · 2026-05-29

**D-04. Enemy design rules** — (a) **No enemy levels** — each troop is one fixed, intricately-tuned design; only towers tier up. (b) **Shared/cumulative rosters** — troops recur across worlds with the same design; the campaign mixes them per-map. (c) Transformations (e.g. Monk→Templar at 80% path) are one-way **archetype swaps**, not levels. (d) **Every ability ships its own FX sprite.** · 2026-05-29

**D-05. Combat/world invariants** — (a) **Towers have no HP** — enemies only stun/disable/debuff them, never damage/destroy. (b) **Buildings have no HP and can't be targeted** (capture/objective only). (c) **Flyers land and walk while carrying a gem.** (d) **All desert maps are oases with waterway lanes** for flight. (e) **No screen-clearing AoE** — every area effect (towers and enchants alike) has a **bounded radius**; nothing wipes the whole screen/map at once. · 2026-05-29

**D-06. (Q-03) Animation density** — **Full for troops; towers are static.** Every **troop** gets idle + locomotion (walk/ride/fly) + attack + death + ability/cast, **4–6 frames each**. **Towers stay locked in place — attack animation only** (no idle bounce, no locomotion, no death; towers have no HP per D-05, so the only extra state is an optional **stun/disabled overlay**). **Every tower tier ships its own attack animation + a custom projectile sprite** — no projectile/beam is reused across tiers (12 per faction; Demon's per-tier beam is its equivalent). **Amended by D-23:** Orc & Goblin towers mount a rotating 8-way directional turret on a base that still never moves (locomotion stays banned; only the turret turns). **Amended by D-25:** troops **drop the attack animation** — idle + locomotion (+fly) (+ability/cast) + death only. · 2026-05-29

**D-07. (Q-22) In-game UI layout** — classic tower-defense arrangement on a **dark background**: **map centred**, **resources top**, **wave queue right**, **enchants + tower-placing bottom**. Distinctive twist: every UI element is an **independent, raised 3D block** with a small bevel/shadow (old-style arcade-button look), floating separately rather than flush-aligned into one panel. · 2026-05-29

**D-08. (Q-01) Art style** — **painted 2D pixel art, ink-line gothic, strongly visible borders** (locked directly in QUESTIONS.md Q-01); **source 48×48 here → superseded by D-21 to 64×64 (2× cell density)**. · 2026-05-29

**D-09. Gem-carry** — **Hybrid.** Any enemy reaching the cave grabs a gem; **specialists excel** (Golden Prince, Gem Shade). While carrying: **slow + flyers land**, armor retained (D-05). Dropped on death (re-grabbable; a carrier escaping = gem lost). Specialist overrides: Golden Prince dismounts → also loses armor; Gem Shade blinks toward the cave. · 2026-05-29

**D-10. Summon control** — **Interval + field cap, phased.** Every summoner has a summon interval **and** a max-alive cap (refills as summons die). Bosses are **phased summoners**: a `Phase` = `{maintainedType, interval, cap, onEnterBurst}`, flipped by HP thresholds. **Burst↔cap rule = Phase reconfigure:** crossing a threshold fires the one-time burst **and** sets the maintained type, so the burst **seeds the new phase's cap** and the **maintained type backfills** as units die — one cap per boss, no double-stacking (so a differing burst type, e.g. the Chief Rebel's 2 Behemoths, lands once and Sand Raiders backfill). **Exception:** the Demon Shadow King summons via **Cursed Portals, each its own summoner with a per-portal cap**. Void Priest resurrects count toward his own cap (2). Concrete per-boss trees live in `SYSTEMS.md` → *Summon trees (per-boss)*. · 2026-05-29

**D-11. Damage & status model** — **Typed resistance table.** Damage types: **PHYSICAL, MAGIC, FIRE, FROST, LIGHTNING, CURSE** (HOLY **cut** — no player-side source). Each troop carries a resistance map (type → resist %; immune = 100 %, vulnerable = negative); existing `armor`/`magicResist` fold in as the PHYSICAL/MAGIC entries. A **separate status-immunity set** covers FEAR / SLOW / STUN / BURN(DoT) / CURSE. Tower→type: Orc=PHYSICAL, Crypt=FROST + CURSE/fear (+ LIGHTNING terminal), Demon=FIRE, Goblin=PHYSICAL (engineering). **MAGIC has no player-tower source — enemy magic-resist = resist vs the Crypt school (FROST/CURSE/LIGHTNING); see D-17.** · 2026-05-29

**D-12. Art consistency** — **all troops within a world share one visual sub-style** (silhouette language, palette, detailing), so each world reads as a coherent faction. Cross-world returning units keep their design but adopt the host world's accents where noted (e.g. desert Outrider = camel-rider). **Clarified by D-28:** the shared sub-style is a palette + detailing *language* only — every unit is still its own distinct creature (no shared prototype body). · 2026-05-29

**D-13. Enchants** — player's active abilities, **6 total**: **Meteor** (FIRE; its lingering **lava pool** is a *skills* upgrade, not on the base enchant), **Fear**, **Freeze**, **Gem Ward**, **Cursed Tribute**, **Dark Overcharge**. **Cost = mana only, no cooldowns** (regen-gated; mana shown top per D-07). **Loadout: the player equips exactly 2** per map (bottom bar). Cut from the draft: Storm Call, Hex, Unveil, Disenchant, Grave Seal. A separate **skills** meta-progression upgrades enchants — **now locked (D-14)**. · 2026-05-29

**D-14. (skills) Skills meta-progression** — **4 faction skill trees** (Orc · Crypt · Demon · Goblin), **all open from the start**, permanent between-map progression on the existing `Skill` / `SkillTreeBonus` / `SkillTreeScreen` seam (refit, not rebuilt). **Revised 2026-05-30:** each tree = **10 skills, every skill has 5 tiers (ranks)** → **40 skills / 200 ranks**; **no ultimate capstone** (the earlier 8-ranked-plus-1-ultimate / 36-node draft is aborted). Skills are **mechanically rich, not flat damage/fire-rate sticks** — levers are drops & economy (coins/scrap/mana-potions, tree-cutting), procs (crit + crit-stun, jam, freeze-chance, %-max-HP execute), deploys (proximity mines, attacking cave-charges), debuffs (armor-pierce, fire-resist-down, burn contagion) and enchant upgrades; the few damage lines are conditional (vs-champion/boss, on-crit, anti-air, on-kill fire-rate). **No separate Sorcery/Greed tree:** every tree folds in its own tower buffs, economy nodes, and **enchant upgrades** (D-13). Enchant→faction map (refined): **Demon → Meteor** (incl. the **lava-pool unlock**), **Crypt → Freeze + Fear**, **Orc → Cursed Tribute**, **Goblin → Gem Ward + Dark Overcharge** — an enchant only upgrades if you invest its faction (this swaps the earlier mapping: Cursed Tribute Goblin→Orc to sit with Orc's coin economy, Dark Overcharge Orc→Goblin to sit with Goblin's tower-machinery). Thematic split: **Orc & Goblin = gold/economy**, **Crypt & Demon = mana/spell**. Points ≈ +1/map +1/star (~100 over 25 maps); maxing all four costs more, forcing a focus. **Witch & Beast skill trees deleted** (D-01). Node icons reuse `UI_ICONS` + the 6 enchant icons; **no bespoke art needed** (ultimates cut). Full node tables in `SYSTEMS.md`. · 2026-05-29, revised 2026-05-30

**D-15. (flight) Flight & air-lane pathing** — **all flyers are water-bound.** A flyer follows an **air lane = a route over water tiles only**, never over land, until the gem cave. **Model (OO):** water tiles are the source of truth (an `airTraversable` flag from the terrain water layer, D-05d); an **`AirRouteProvider`** turns them into a route — default **`WaterPathfinder`** (cheap grid A\*/BFS over water tiles, spawn→cave, computed once + cached) emitting a smoothed **polyline**, with an optional **`AuthoredPolyline`** hand-drawn override. Both feed the **same `AirFollower`**, a swappable `MovementStrategy` on `Enemy` (sibling of `GroundFollower`). **On gem-grab the flyer lands** → strategy swaps to `GroundFollower`, snaps to the cave's ground-path node, walks out slow + targetable (D-05c, D-09). **Every tower hits air** — flying is never a target gate; Goblin's anti-air skill is a *bonus vs flying* only. **Threat = speed, not distance** — flyer archetypes carry high move speed (per-troop tuned, D-04); lanes may be any length. **Authoring:** flyer maps paint a water route connecting spawn→cave (author-time validator + straight-line fallback if broken); the **cave mouth sits adjacent to a water endpoint**. Covers Seraph (W2), Giant Vulture (W3), Clockwork Sentinel (W4) — the Sentinel's earlier free-air reading is dropped, it rides water like the rest. **Revised 2026-05-30 → D-15a (W5 + roster completion):** flyers per world are **W1 none · W2 Seraph · W3 Giant Vulture · W4 Royal Falconer + Clockwork Sentinel · W5 Imp Swarm**; every world that fields a flyer needs a water route. **W5 Shadow Realm uses a *cursed waterway*** — void-tainted water (AQ deep under a CR curse tint + HX glow, IK void edges): it reads as a void rift but is **water-typed**, so it carries `airTraversable` exactly like W2–W4. Water is thus the **universal** flight medium in every biome, the Shadow Realm included; this closes the brief §6.4 [OPEN] and the W2 confirm. · 2026-05-30

**D-17. (troops) Troop ↔ D-11 resistance binding** — the **53 archetypes** are bound to the typed-resistance system in `TROOPS.md` (qualitative **bands**, tuned to % in implementation; no new troops, no levels — D-04). **MAGIC clarified:** no player tower deals generic MAGIC (Orc/Goblin=PHYSICAL, Crypt=FROST/CURSE/LIGHTNING, Demon=FIRE, HOLY cut — D-11), so **enemy "magic-resist" = resist vs the Crypt school (FROST/CURSE/LIGHTNING)**; unit-specific FROST/CURSE resists stack on top. Derived the OO way from **keyword + role conventions** (add a troop → tag its keywords → resistances fall out): *keyword* — "fearless"→FEAR-immune (aura emitters self-immune while active); "immune to all slow"→SLOW+FREEZE-immune; "fire-immune"→FIRE-immune+BURN-immune; "mechanical"→BURN-immune (+LIGHTNING-vuln where flagged). *role* — **boss = FEAR+STUN+execute-immune, SLOW-resist** (one life unless noted); **champion = execute-immune + STUN-resist**; **mounted gem-carrier = dynamic armor** (fortified mounted → none after the on-grab dismount, D-09). Flavour: **Imp Swarm = FIRE-resist** (demonic, not immune, so Demon stays relevant); **Dune Behemoth stays fully CC-able** (only Abyssal Behemoth is fear-immune). · 2026-05-30

**D-18. (implementation) Build approach — full from-scratch rebuild.** *Revised 2026-05-30 (was "full engine-extension").* The code audit (enemy entity = `Hero`; `Hero.damage(int, boolean isMagic)` flat armor/MR subtraction; only slow+fear status; `BossBehavior`/`EnemyAbility` hardcoded switches; `Faction` still carries Witch/Beast; old rosters in `HeroType`/`TowerType`; `*V2Data` sprites auto-generated) proved the README's *"engine stays the same, only assets change"* no longer holds — the locked docs (D-09 … D-17) outgrew the flat combat engine. **Chosen (revised): rewrite the whole game from scratch.** The old `cursed_treasure_v2` build is **deleted** — everything except the app icon and the `redesign/` docs — and the rebuild realises the locked docs directly rather than refitting legacy code. The audit + the deleted code serve **only as reference for what the rebuild must reproduce**; since there is **no VCS** to recover anything (OneDrive only), whatever lives only in code and the rebuild still needs — notably the **menu/screen flow** — must be captured into `redesign/` *before* deletion (or explicitly chosen to be redesigned fresh). The **OO target architecture is unchanged:** typed `DamageType` + `ResistanceProfile` + `DamagePacket` with `Hero.damage(packet)` (replaces the binary `magicDamage`); `StatusController` + per-unit immunity `EnumSet` (D-17); a `UnitBehavior` Strategy library replacing the `EnemyAbility`/`BossBehavior` switches; data-driven `SummonTree` (D-10); `MovementStrategy` + water-air-route (D-15); a `ResistanceProfileFactory` that derives the matrix from keyword/role flags (TROOPS §1). **Build:** a clean project skeleton + frozen Phase-0 interface contract → parallel agent teams (Combat Core · Behavior · Towers · Troops · Art · UI · Maps/Waves + continuous QA). Full gap table, architecture, build order, risks, and open items in `IMPLEMENTATION.md`; the architecture is drafted and **pending review**. · 2026-05-29, revised to from-scratch 2026-05-30

**D-19. (Q-04) Map tile philosophy** — **full auto-edge (autotiling).** The engine auto-selects edge/corner tile variants from a per-biome **bitmask set**; the Map Builder paints terrain by **role** (base · path · obstacle · special) and the engine resolves which edge art to draw. Design authors a **full transition set per biome** once (base↔path + base↔special borders), reused across all 25 maps; obstacles stay **static connectable clusters**. Chosen over static hand-placement (too manual across 25 maps) and hybrid (half-measure) for the cleanest blends, fewest hand placements, and best expandability. Ripples: `INVENTORY §6` / `STYLEGUIDE §4` terrain sets become autotile ramps; the from-scratch engine needs a neighbour-aware tile resolver. · 2026-05-30

**D-20. (Q-05) Tier visual progression** — **structural, not cosmetic.** Each of the 12 tiers per faction is a **visibly different building**, not a recolour/glow of the same silhouette: tiers escalate by **rebuilding the structure** (bigger frame, more barrels/limbs, added machinery, a changed "business end") within the faction's shape-grammar + material. Rationale: towers are **static** (D-06), so the silhouette + visible weapon is the *only* readability channel — cosmetic-only tiers would be indistinguishable in play. Operationalised by the **per-tower weapon-armament spec** (design packet `instructions for claude design/TOWERS.md` → *"Weapon armament — the per-tower distinctness spec"*), which assigns each of the 48 towers a distinct armament so a faction's 12 read as 12 distinct silhouettes at 32 px. **Extended by D-27:** ≥5 per-tier levers + per-faction shape grammar + no shared cross-faction decoration. · 2026-05-31

**D-21. (Q-34) Source-art resolution — ×2-of-cell (64-based) density.** Author every world-grid sprite at **64 px per 32 px logical cell** (2× density), downscaled for display — crisp up to a 2× / hi-DPI screen, with the **no-AA indexed rule (D-08) intact** (source density ≈ display density; **no** supersample-then-downscale, which would blend pixels off-palette). The **logical grid is unchanged** — still **32×30 cells @ 32 px**, world **1024×960 logical** (README non-negotiable); only the *source canvas* doubles. Canvas = on-screen cell-footprint × 64: **tiles 64×64 · troops 64×64 · large troops 64×96 · air actors 64×64 · towers 64×128 · projectiles ≤64×64 · gems 64×64 · particles ≤32×32 · ability/enchant FX ≤192×192 · buildings ≤192×192**; UI/screen art doubles likewise (**icons 48×48 · world cards 512×384 · map nodes 192×192 · backgrounds 2560×1440 · title splash 1536×384**). Cost ≈ 1.8–4× the prior 48-based atlas/authoring; chosen for a crisper retro-HD read. Authoritative table: `instructions for claude design/claude_design_instructions.md` §2.2. **Scope = uniform:** every drawn asset is 2× (sprites · tiles · chrome · icons · backgrounds · FX · particles) — never mix densities under FitViewport; **text/font is the only exception** (governed by Q-21). **Payoff is conditional — kept as the safe default (render-res undecided 2026-05-31):** 2× only stays crisp if the 1280×720 logical frame is displayed at **≥2× physical px** (≥1440p / hi-DPI / fullscreen); if the game ships **pinned to native 720p**, the no-AA downscale discards the extra pixels → **revert to 1× (32-based) before mass authoring**. **Caveat:** this is a *density/crispness* lever, **not** a distinctness fix — distinctness still rests on silhouette + faction shape-grammar + the weapon armature (D-20). · 2026-05-31

**D-22. (palette) Darker palette — global darkening of D-16.** The whole game skews **darker** than the STYLEGUIDE §1 first-pass hexes (a direct art directive — already given to Claude design; refines D-16's cool gothic-night, family/step *structure* unchanged). Direction — author by **CODE**, apply as a `palette.js` hex retune (**no sprite re-authoring**): (a) **deepen the stage/backdrop** to IK1/IK0 (`#0a0612`/`#07040d`) as the dominant field, IK3 reserved for raised chrome faces; (b) **bias fills one step darker** — large areas take the darker ramp neighbour; reserve the top 1–2 light steps for highlight/rim only; (c) **saturated brights are accents, not fields** — EM/IC/VL/HX/GD read as glints/FX/faction accents that **pop** against the darkened mass (sharpens D-16's "accents pop"); (d) **protect D-08** — borders stay well-visible: keep a value gap between fill and the IK0/IK1 outline, and rim-light any dark silhouette sitting on the dark backdrop so it never muddies. · 2026-05-31

**D-23. (towers) Orc & Goblin towers — static base + rotating directional turret.** *Amends D-06 for two of the four factions.* **Orc and Goblin** towers split into a **static base** (the structure, one sprite per tier) **+ a rotating turret** — the "business end" / visible weapon of D-20 — that **aims at the current target**. **Crypt and Demon are unchanged:** single static sprite (their fire is orbiting charges, beams, and novas with no tracking barrel, so a turret would add cost for no readability gain). **8-way directional, not free rotation:** the turret is authored as **5 hand-drawn 3/4 facings** (E, NE, SE, N, S) and **mirrored** to the other three (W, NW, SW) = 8 facings; the engine **snaps `aimAngle` to the nearest of 8**. Chosen over continuous 2D rotation because the no-AA ink-line gothic art (D-08) looks flat/muddy when rotated to arbitrary angles — discrete painted facings keep the gothic read. **The base stays locked in place** (D-06's "no locomotion" holds); only the turret turns, and the **attack animation plays on the turret in its current facing**. *(Distinct from projectiles, which still free-rotate to flight angle per §2.2 — small fast bolts tolerate it; a large static-viewed turret does not.)* **Engine:** `Tower` gains an optional `turret` sub-sprite + a `pivot` (turret seat on the base) + `aimAngle`; towers with no turret render exactly as before. **Cost:** +24 turret sprite sets (Orc 12 + Goblin 12), each ~5 facings × the attack frames; Crypt/Demon add nothing. The **48 silhouette count is unchanged** — the turret is a sub-component of an Orc/Goblin tower, not a new tower. **Amended by D-26:** Goblin's **drone & bomber lines are launch platforms (no turret)** — the turret covers Orc (all 12) + Goblin's 6 gun/ordnance towers (≈18 turret sets, not 24). · 2026-05-31

Engine: `UiPalette.factionAccent` → `ORC`=MS green, `GOBLIN`=BR brass (was the reverse). · 2026-06-01

**D-26. **D-27.
